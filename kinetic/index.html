Strength floor

Machines that build power

Every machine is designed to help you train with control, confidence, and consistency. Start light, learn the movement pattern, and progressively add load as your form stays sharp.

Cardio

Treadmill

How to use: Step onto the belt, hold the rails for balance, then start at a low speed and gradually increase. Keep your posture tall, brace your core, and use the incline to build endurance without overstriding.

Lower body

Leg Press

How to use: Sit with your back flat against the pad, place feet shoulder-width apart, and lower the platform until your knees track naturally. Press through your midfoot and avoid locking the knees at the top.

Strength

Cable Machine

How to use: Set the pulley height to match the motion, grab the handle with a firm grip, and maintain a stable torso. Pull through the full range of motion and control the return so the movement stays smooth and consistent.

Conditioning

Rowing Machine

How to use: Sit tall, connect the feet, and drive with your legs before leaning back and pulling with the arms. Keep the stroke efficient: legs first, then torso, then arms, with a controlled finish.
